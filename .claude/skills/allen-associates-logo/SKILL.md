# Allen & Associates — Official Logo

The official logo: a blue-and-green cross mark beside "ALLEN & ASSOCIATES" (green/blue), "INSURANCE AGENCY" (green), and the tagline "MAKING MEDICARE EASY" (dark gray).

## Rules

- Never recolor, stretch, crop, or redraw the logo; scale it proportionally only.
- The tagline is dark gray, so it disappears on dark backgrounds. On a dark or busy background (e.g., the navy video background), place the logo on a white rounded plate with padding of at least 10% of the logo height.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "INSURANCE AGENCY" line.
- Minimum width: 240 px in video/social, 1.5 in in print, so the tagline stays legible.
- The logo already contains the tagline "Making Medicare Easy"; don't repeat it as separate text right next to the logo.
- Agency contact details used alongside the logo: phone `800-324-9986`, web `allenandassociates.agency`.
